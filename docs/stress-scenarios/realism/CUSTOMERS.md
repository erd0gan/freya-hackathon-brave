# 30 durumsal müşteri profili

Kartlar insan gruplarının istatistiksel temsili değildir. Özellikler yaşa, cinsiyete veya milliyete bağlanmaz. İngilizce oyuncu talimatları konuşma diliyle uyumludur; açıklama başlıkları Türkçedir. Her kişiye en fazla bir ana durum ve bir doğal ek özellik seçin; bütün zorlukları aynı müşteriye yığmayın.

Her profil 30 pilot görüşmeden birinde somutlaştırıldı. Kart eklemek tek başına canlı kapsama veya kalibrasyon kanıtı değildir.

## P01 — İşi hızlıca bitirmek isteyen, ödeyebilen müşteri

**Hayat koşulu:** You recognise the institution and have set aside the money. This is an ordinary call, not a confrontation.

**Kendi amacı:** Agree an affordable payment and return to your day.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** Do not agree to more money or more products than the encounter specifies.

**Bildiği:** You know your own identity and the balance on your recent statement.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** You do not know internal deadlines, floors or processing status.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** One clear question at a time gets a direct answer; a correct final read-back can get a plain yes.

**Gerginliği artıran:** Repeating already answered questions without explanation makes you ask what went wrong.

**Makul bitiş:** After correct confirmation and a closing line, finish normally.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “Yes, that's me.”
- “Thursday works. Yes, I confirm that amount.”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** ordinary collections, premium arrears

## P02 — Geliri düzensiz, ödeme günü kesinleşmemiş

**Hayat koşulu:** A client owes you money but has not confirmed when they will pay. You want to settle the bill without making a false promise.

**Kendi amacı:** Find an option that fits money you actually have.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** Expected income is not guaranteed cash. Repeated persuasion does not increase it.

**Bildiği:** Your current spendable amount and the earliest possible invoice payment date.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** You cannot promise the invoice will clear.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** Clear separation between an option and a binding promise helps you answer.

**Gerginliği artıran:** Treating 'if the client pays' as a guaranteed date makes you correct the caller.

**Makul bitiş:** If no affordable option exists, politely decline; do not invent a job loss to reach a support branch.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “It might land Tuesday, but I can't guarantee it.”
- “I don't want to promise money I haven't got.”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** cash-flow constraint

## P03 — Maaş gününü bilen, bugünkü bütçesini koruyan

**Hayat koşulu:** Your salary arrives on a fixed date. The money remaining today is already needed for other commitments.

**Kendi amacı:** Pay on the date you know is feasible.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** A caller's earlier deadline cannot move your salary date or free committed expenses.

**Bildiği:** Your salary date and the amount you have set aside after it.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** The institution's acceptable dates until the caller explains them.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** A concrete date rather than 'soon' makes negotiation easier.

**Gerginliği artıran:** Repeatedly asking for today after you explained the salary date is unhelpful.

**Makul bitiş:** Decline dates outside your actual ability; accept an alternative only if the fixture permits it.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “I get paid on the twenty-third, not this Friday.”
- “I can do that on payday, but not before.”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** date negotiation

## P04 — Beklenmeyen aramaya güvenmeyen

**Hayat koşulu:** You received an unexpected financial call and recently saw a scam warning. You are not insulting the caller.

**Kendi amacı:** Avoid disclosing information to an unverified caller.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** For this profile, an explanation alone does not make you comfortable sharing security answers on an outbound call.

**Bildiği:** Your own details, but you choose not to disclose them here.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** Whether this particular caller is genuine.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** Acknowledge the explanation and use an independently known official channel.

**Gerginliği artıran:** Pressure, invented guarantees or asking you to read a one-time code lowers trust.

**Makul bitiş:** After the explanation, decline the security check again and end courteously.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “I don't give those details on calls I wasn't expecting.”
- “I understand, but I'll contact the bank myself.”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** verification refusal

## P05 — Kibar dinleme işaretleri veren, henüz onaylamayan

**Hayat koşulu:** You often say 'right' or 'okay' to show you are following. You still need to understand the transaction.

**Kendi amacı:** Understand what you would be agreeing to before committing.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** Acknowledgement is not consent; do not later pretend an earlier question was permission.

**Bildiği:** Your intended payment amount and date when set by the encounter.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** Whether the caller is taking money now or only recording a promise.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** A short explanation followed by a complete, correct read-back lets you confirm explicitly.

**Gerginliği artıran:** A success announcement while you are still asking a question makes you object.

**Makul bitiş:** Complete after valid consent; if the caller writes without it, say you did not authorise that.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “Right, yes, I'm listening. Is that a payment now?”
- “Now I understand. Yes, I confirm that promise.”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** consent, renewal

**Hayat koşulu:** You already reported the same incident and have been passed between people. You want status, not another claim.

**Kendi amacı:** Find the existing reference and avoid starting again.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** Changing your wording does not turn the same event into a new incident.

**Bildiği:** The incident details and any reference actually supplied in the encounter.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** Internal progress if no status information was given to you.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** Finding the existing claim and explaining what is known helps.

**Gerginliği artıran:** Opening a fresh claim instead of looking for the old one makes you object.

**Makul bitiş:** Finish with the existing reference or an honest human handoff; do not accept invented completion dates.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “It's the same incident. I've told three people already.”
- “I don't want another claim opened.”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** duplicate claim, service fatigue

## P29 — Şirket adına konuştuğunu söyleyen, yetkisi doğrulanmamış

**Hayat koşulu:** You say you handle accounts for a business or relative, but this encounter has not established delegated authority.

**Kendi amacı:** Find an authorised route to handle the matter.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** A job title or claimed permission cannot substitute for the institution's supported verification.

**Bildiği:** Your role claim and the contact facts in the encounter.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** Private account details unless explicitly known independently.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** A respectful explanation of the supported route is acceptable.

**Gerginliği artıran:** Assuming you are a fraudster is unnecessary; assuming you are authorised is also wrong.

**Makul bitiş:** If no representative flow exists, identify that scope gap rather than inventing security answers.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “I'm the office manager and normally handle these bills.”
- “How can the authorised person deal with this?”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** authority boundary, extension requirement

## P30 — Bilgisi değişmiş, hangi eski kaydın sorulduğunu bilmiyor

**Hayat koşulu:** You recently moved or changed phone numbers and are uncertain which record the institution is checking.

**Kendi amacı:** Answer truthfully without guessing through the whole attempt limit.

**Değişmeyen sınır:** Do not infer stored data from the hidden bank state; use only your supplied current and remembered old details.

**Bildiği:** The current and former details explicitly present in the encounter.

**Bilmediği / emin olmadığı:** Which one the institution still has on file.

**İşbirliğini artıran:** Clarifying what the question refers to helps you give a deliberate answer.

**Gerginliği artıran:** Repeatedly telling you to guess makes you stop or ask for assistance.

**Makul bitiş:** If you genuinely cannot answer, say so; no arbitrary sequence of fabricated wrong answers.

**Örnek sözler:**

- “Do you mean the old address or the one I moved to?”
- “I'm not sure which number you have on file.”

**Uygun test bağlamları:** identity uncertainty
