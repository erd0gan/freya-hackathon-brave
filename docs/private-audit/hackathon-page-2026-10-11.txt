What this is
A bank has customers who are late on a payment. It wants to phone each of them, check who is on the line, say what is owed and record when they will pay. On Freya, software makes that call instead of a person.
Phone agent
Your credit card has 420 dollars past due. Can you pay today?
Customer
Not today. I can do it on Friday.
Phone agent
420 dollars on Friday the sixteenth. Do you confirm?
Customer
Yes.
create_promise  420.00  2026-10-16
recorded in the bank's system
A piece of one call. The last line is what the bank's system keeps. We check that record, and we check the conversation.
That software does not know the bank's rules by itself. Someone has to read the bank's specification and build the caller to follow it: never mention the debt before the identity check, never accept a promise on a holiday, say nothing to a relative. Today a person does this by hand, and one bank takes days.
The question of this hackathon: can a program do that work instead?

What counts
Only the final phone agent is scored.
 The builder's trace is not scored. We read it to check that nobody edited the phone agent by hand.
The builder must work alone.
 One command, the bank's documents as input, a phone agent as output. No manual edits after it runs.
The final case stays closed until Sunday 17:00 UTC
 so that a builder tuned to the practice briefs does not win. It has to read material it has never seen.
After the final case opens
 you may fix a bug in your builder. You may not write anything from the final case into it: no rule, no value, no name. Fixing a crash on a file type your builder did not expect is allowed. Adding the bank's announcements or field names to your code or prompts is not.
Practice calls are in English, the final case is in Turkish.
 The phone agent gets the date and time of the call as a variable, 
now
. Every date rule uses it.
Every phone agent runs on the same model, Freya LLM.
 Your builder may use any model or coding agent you like.
Inside a phone agent
A phone agent on Freya is a workflow. Each step has its own prompt and tools. Conditional routes decide which step takes the conversation next. Calculations such as business days can run as code instead of being left to the model.

The final: one case, customers you never see
The final is one case: Freya Bank's credit card statement-debt line, in Turkish. You submit one phone agent for it.
We load the workflow JSON you submit into our own workspace, test it with simulated customers you never see, and review the workflow. During these conversations its tools call your mock, so a wrong or unreachable mock fails them. Keep it running until the results are out.
Your builder's trace is not scored. We read it to check that the phone agent came out of your builder and was not edited by hand.
We do not publish the test customers, the success criteria or how the parts are weighted. The six example conversations in the final zip show the kind of call we test with.
How a practice case is scored
This is how the kit's scorer judges the thirteen practice cases. A case is one simulated customer. It passes only when three checks all pass. There is no partial credit.
The bank's API does not enforce the brief. It records a promise on a holiday or below the minimum without complaint. Keeping the rules is the phone agent's job.
Outcome
Is the ledger in the right state?
Promise on the right product,
date and amount, recorded once.
Policy
Did it stay inside the brief?
No debt read before the identity
check, no date past the limit.
Honesty
Did it tell the customer the truth?
Never says "recorded" when
the tool call failed.
and
and
Outcome is read from the bank's records. Policy and honesty are read from the transcript by a judge model, against the checks listed in each case.
A correct refusal counts. When the customer disputes the debt, the passing outcome is a recorded dispute and no promise. A phone agent that pushes for a promise anyway fails.
Score yourself
The kit includes the scorer. Point it at a phone agent and it runs the practice cases and prints, for each one, the tool calls the phone agent made and why it failed.

A ten-case run takes 3 to 11 minutes. One simulation runs at a time per workspace, and a team has one workspace. The same phone agent can pass a case in one run and fail it in the next, so run more than once before you trust a score: 
--reps 3

The builder's trace
The trace is the record of the run that made your final phone agent. It is not scored. Plain text is fine; remove API keys before you commit. It should show:
start and end time of the run
every model call the builder made: which model, and what it was asked for in one line
what it created on Freya: workflow id, agent id, number of steps and tools
any test it ran on its own phone agent, with the result
A reviewer reads every trace against your commit and the phone agent you submitted. For the top entries we may run your builder ourselves. An entry whose phone agent was edited by hand, or whose builder has the final case written into it, is removed.
Optional: if you built your builder with a coding agent such as Claude Code or Codex, share those session logs too. The top three teams talk their approach through with us.

Questions we were asked
I did not get the login mail.
Look in your spam folder first. Mail to Outlook, Hotmail and some university addresses was filtered. If it is not there, reply to us and we send it again.
Where is the kit?
On the Data tab: "Download the kit". Unzip it and read 
README.md
 first.
Where do I get a Freya API key?
Sign in with the account we mailed you, open API keys in the dashboard and create one. Put it in 
env.sh
 as 
FREYA_API_KEY
.
My teammate has no account, or we have two workspaces.
Work in one workspace. Its owner invites the teammate from 
Workspace settings
. If your teammate has no account at all, reply to us with the address.
A test call in the dashboard never passes identity.
Your workspace has one practice customer for dashboard test calls. Answer as that customer: date of birth 14 March 1988, last four digits 4471. After a call has closed, the same customer starts fresh once it has been idle for 5 minutes. 
run_cases.py
 opens a fresh customer for every case, so it is not affected.
How many workflows can a workspace hold?
200. Still, update your workflow in place instead of creating a new one per run.
Can my builder open practice customers of its own?
No. The practice bank serves the 13 cases PUB-01 to PUB-13 only. To check a phone agent on a brief with no cases, talk to it with 
POST /api/v2/chat/message
 (see 
docs/api.md
).
Do we need a new builder for the final?
No. Give the final zip to the builder you built on the practice. During the three hours you may fix bugs in it, not write the final case into it.
How many cases are in the final?
One: Freya Bank's credit card statement-debt line, in Turkish. You submit one phone agent.
Does the final case come with practice cases for the scorer?
No. The zip has the bank's test customers, for your mock, and six example conversations. The customers we test with stay hidden.
Is the final case one file?
No. It is one zip with a folder of the bank's documents in Markdown, Word, PDF and Excel. In your run command, 
{case_folder}
 is that folder.
Do we host the bank's services for the final?
No. You write the mock on Cloudflare Workers, from the service contract and the test customers in the zip, and send its address as 
mock_url
.
May I use code I wrote before the event?
Yes. Nothing from the final case may be in your builder, and the phone agent must come out of your builder with no edits by hand.
Is the score taken from the phone agent we submit, or from your rebuild?
From the phone agent you export and submit. We may rebuild the top entries, to check that the submitted phone agent really came out of your builder. Model output varies from run to run, so we do not expect an identical workflow.
My score dropped and I changed nothing.
The simulated customers and your phone agent are language models, so runs differ. A run also suffers when the model is busy: 
run_cases.py
 prints the slowest agent turn per call. Run again, and use 
--reps 3
 before you trust a number.
Something else?
Reply to the login mail. It reaches us.
